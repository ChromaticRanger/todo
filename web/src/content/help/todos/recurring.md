---
title: Recurring todos
summary: Repeat tasks on any cadence — the next one appears when you finish this one.
plan: free
order: 5
---
When creating or editing a todo, set **Repeat** to any interval of days or months. That covers all the usual cadences — every 1 day for daily, every 7 days for weekly, every 14 for fortnightly, every 1 month, every 12 months for yearly — plus anything in between, like "every 3 days" or "every 6 months".

Recurring todos work on a *spawn-on-completion* model: when you complete one, the **next occurrence is created automatically**, dated one cadence ahead (and always in the future — if you finish something late, the next one won't be born already overdue).

That means only one occurrence exists at a time, and skipping a repeat is as simple as completing (or deleting) the current one.
