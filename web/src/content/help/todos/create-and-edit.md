title: Creating and editing todos
summary: Add tasks with a title, description, category, priority and due date.
plan: free
order: 1
---
Click **Add → Todo** in the header (or press **Alt+T**). A todo needs a title; everything else is optional:

- **Description** — extra detail, shown on the card
- **List and category** — where it lives; type a new category name to create one on the fly
- **Priority** — Low, Medium or High
- **Due date & time** — when it needs doing
- **Repeat** — make it recurring

![The todo form](/help-images/todo-form.png)

To change any of these later, click the **pencil (Edit)** icon on the item. To remove it, use the **bin (Delete)** icon — if you have *Confirm before deleting* switched on in Settings, you'll be asked first.
