---
title: Snoozing todos
summary: Hide an item until later — it comes back exactly when you choose.
plan: free
order: 6
---
Not ready to deal with something? Click the **clock (Remind me later)** icon on a todo and pick the date it should **reappear on**. You can optionally reschedule its due date at the same time.

![The Remind me later dialog](/help-images/snooze-dialog.png)

Until that date, the item is hidden from the **All** view so it stops nagging you. It still appears in the time-based views (Today / Week / Month / Overdue) so a deadline can't silently vanish.

To bring one back early, find it in a time-based view and open **Remind me later** again — there's an **Unsnooze** button — or use the snooze toggle when editing the item.
