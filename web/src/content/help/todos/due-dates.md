---
title: Due dates and times
summary: Give items a deadline — with a specific time of day if you want one.
plan: free
order: 3
---
Any todo can carry a **due date**, and optionally a specific **time of day**. The exact time you set is always honoured and displayed. (Due dates are a todo thing — events have start times instead, and bookmarks and notes have neither.)

Due dates drive the time-windowed views: a todo due before midnight tonight appears in **Today**, within seven days in **Week**, within thirty in **Month**, and anything past-due lands in **Overdue**.

If you'd like a nudge as well, Settings → Daily reminders can show a "due today" pop-up when you sign in, notify you when an item falls due, and send a daily email digest.
