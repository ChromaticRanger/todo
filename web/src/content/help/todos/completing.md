---
title: Completing todos
summary: Tick tasks off, undo them, and review what you've done.
plan: free
order: 4
---
Click the **circle** on a todo to mark it complete. It records a completion timestamp and moves out of your pending views.

Completed items aren't gone: the **Completed** view lists everything you've finished over a selectable window (last 7 / 30 / 90 days, 1 year, or all time). Clicking a completed item's tick (**Mark pending**) restores it to your list.

Completing a **recurring** todo automatically creates its next occurrence — see *Recurring todos*.
