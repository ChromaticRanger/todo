---
title: Viewers and editors
summary: What each role can do, and how to change or remove someone.
plan: pro
order: 2
---
Every person you invite has one of two roles, which you pick when you invite them and can change at any time.

- **Add and edit items** (editor) — they can create, edit, complete, snooze and delete items in the list, and reorder them. They cannot rename or delete the list itself, and they cannot invite anyone else.
- **View only** (viewer) — they see everything in the list and any changes as they happen, but can't alter it.

**To change someone's role**, open the share dialog from the list tab and pick a different option beside their name. It takes effect immediately, including for anyone who has the list open.

**To remove someone**, use the remove button beside their name. They lose access straight away. Nothing they added is deleted — those items belong to the list, which is yours.

**To stop sharing entirely**, remove everyone. The list and all its contents stay exactly as they are; only the access goes.

One thing worth knowing: items an editor adds are stored against **your** account, so they count towards your plan's limits rather than theirs.
