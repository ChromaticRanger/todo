---
title: Working on a shared list
summary: How live updates, shared-item marking and leaving a list work.
plan: free
order: 3
---
A list someone has shared with you appears as a tab alongside your own, marked with a people icon and a coloured left edge so you can tell it apart at a glance. Hovering the icon tells you whose list it is.

**Changes arrive live.** When anyone on the list adds, edits or completes something, everyone else sees it without refreshing. If your connection drops, the list catches up by itself when it comes back.

**Shared items stay marked wherever they turn up.** Dated items from a shared list mix into your own **Today**, **Week** and **Month** views alongside your personal ones, so each carries a marker showing whose list it came from. You are never left wondering why an unfamiliar task is in your day.

**What you can do** depends on the role you were given — see *Viewers and editors*. If you can edit, your additions belong to the list rather than to you, which means they stay there if you later leave.

**To leave a shared list**, hover its tab and choose to leave. You lose access until someone invites you again; nothing is deleted. The owner can also remove you, with the same result.

On the **Free plan** you can be a member of one shared list at a time. Sharing a list of your own requires Pro.
