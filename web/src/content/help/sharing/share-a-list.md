---
title: Sharing a list with someone
summary: Invite someone by email to work on the same list as you.
plan: pro
order: 1
---
Sharing gives another person access to one of your lists. They see everything in it, and everything added to it later — it stays one list, not a copy.

> This is different from **publishing to Discover**, which puts a snapshot of a list into the public catalogue for strangers to clone. Sharing is private, live, and between named people.

**To share a list**, hover its tab and click the people icon, then enter an email address and choose what they can do.

- **They don't need an account yet.** If the address has never signed up, they get a link that walks them through creating one and lands them on the list.
- **Invitations expire**, and you can resend or revoke one at any time from the same dialog.
- The invitation says who invited them and which list — it never exposes your other lists.

**Sharing a list is part of Pro.** Accepting an invitation is free: someone on the Free plan can be a member of **one** shared list, which is enough to be invited into a friend's holiday planning without needing to subscribe first.

If you downgrade from Pro, the lists you've already shared keep working for everyone on them. You just can't send new invitations until you upgrade again.
