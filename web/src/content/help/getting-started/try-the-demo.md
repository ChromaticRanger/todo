Click **"Open the demo"** on the landing page to start a demo session — no account required.

The demo gives you a private, pre-seeded workspace with **Pro-level access for 30 minutes**, so you can try everything including the calendar, search and Discover. A timer shows how long you have left.

If you like it, sign up before the timer runs out: choosing **Pro** keeps everything you made during the demo; choosing **Free** starts you fresh with a clean workspace.

**Already signed up?** The demo runs as its own temporary account, so starting it signs you out of yours while it runs — nothing is lost, and you can sign back in as soon as you're done. You'll find a *"Preview Pro in the 30-minute demo"* link on the plan-selection page, or you can upgrade directly from your account page at any time.
