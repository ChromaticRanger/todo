---
title: Creating an account
summary: Sign up with email and password, then verify your address.
plan: free
order: 1
---
Sign up from the landing page with your email address and a password, or sign in with Google or GitHub.

After signing up with email you'll receive a **verification email** — click the link inside it to confirm your address. Your verification status is always visible on your account page.

Once you're in, you'll be asked to choose a plan (Free or Pro) before you land in the app. You can change this later at any time.
