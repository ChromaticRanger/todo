Right after sign-up you choose between **Free** and **Pro**.

**Free** includes all core task management — todos, bookmarks, notes, categories, every list view, snoozing, recurring tasks and the browser extension — capped at **3 lists and 50 items** in total. A banner warns you as you approach the caps.

**Pro** removes both caps and unlocks:

- The **calendar** (Overall Schedule) and time-blocked **events**
- **Global search** (Ctrl/⌘K)
- **Discover** — publishing, browsing and cloning community lists
- **Bookmark import** from a browser export file

Pro is available monthly or annually. You can start on Free and upgrade from your account page whenever you like — nothing is lost either way.
