The **Overall Schedule** (calendar icon in the header) lays out your commitments on a real calendar:

![The Overall Schedule in month view](/help-images/calendar.png)

- **Month view** for the shape of the weeks ahead; **week view** with a 24-hour grid for the fine detail. Your preferred view is remembered.
- **Events** render as time blocks sized by their duration; all-day events sit in a top row.
- Entries are **colour-coded** (with a per-item colour palette available), and clicking a todo jumps back to it in its list.
- Navigate week-to-week or month-to-month, and jump back to today at any time.

Items found via global search that live on the calendar open here, on the right date, with recurring events landing on their next occurrence.
