---
title: Recurring events
summary: Weekly, monthly or yearly series, with an optional end date.
plan: pro
order: 3
---
An event can repeat **weekly, monthly or yearly**, with an optional *repeat until* date to end the series.

Unlike recurring todos (which spawn their next occurrence when you complete one), a recurring event is stored as a **single series** that the calendar expands into occurrences automatically as you browse — edit the series and every future occurrence reflects the change.

Individual occurrences can be adjusted where you need a one-off variation, and global search jumps to a series' **next upcoming occurrence**.
