Events are calendar entries — unlike todos they have a **start time and duration** rather than a deadline, and they live on the calendar rather than in a list.

Create one with **Add → Event**, or simply **drag on empty space** in the calendar's time grid to block out the slot directly. An event has a title, optional description, a start, an end (or **All day**), an optional colour, and an optional repeat.

![The event form](/help-images/event-form.png)

Events can't be "completed" — they simply occur. Recurring series expand automatically into the calendar as you navigate — see *Recurring events*.
