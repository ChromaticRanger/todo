title: Global search
summary: Press Ctrl/⌘K and find anything you've ever saved.
plan: pro
order: 1
---
Press **Ctrl+K** (Windows/Linux) or **⌘K** (Mac) — or click the magnifier in the header — and start typing. Search covers **everything you've saved**: todos, bookmarks, notes and calendar events, matching against titles, descriptions and URLs.

![Search results with matched text highlighted](/help-images/search-modal.png)

Results are relevance-ranked (title matches first) and labelled by type. Selecting one **jumps straight to the item**: list items open in their list with the card highlighted; events open the calendar on the right date, with a recurring series landing on its next occurrence.

If a result is currently snoozed, you'll get a one-click **Unsnooze** offer instead of a silent dead end.
