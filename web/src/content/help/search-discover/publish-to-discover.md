---
title: Publishing a list to Discover
summary: Share a list with the community catalogue.
plan: pro
order: 2
---
Any of your lists can be published to **Discover**, the community catalogue. When publishing you choose:

- A **display name** and **description** for the catalogue entry
- An **emoji** to give it a face
- A **category** so it's browsable

Publishing shares a snapshot-style copy for others to browse and clone — your original list stays yours, and you can unpublish later. Personal metadata like snoozes and completion history isn't part of what's shared.
