title: Reporting a list
summary: Flag a published list that shouldn't be in the catalogue.

---
Anything published to **Discover** is visible to everyone browsing it, so there's a **Report** button on each community list — on the card and on the list itself.

Reporting asks for a reason:

- **Spam or advertising**
- **Offensive or harmful**
- **Broken or misleading links**
- **Something else**

You can add a note if there's something specific worth pointing at. Reports go to the Stash Squirrel team, and the publisher isn't told who reported them.

You can report a given list once. Reporting it again changes nothing — it doesn't add weight, and you don't need to.

**What happens next.** A reported list stays in the catalogue until someone looks at it; a report on its own doesn't hide anything, so one person can't take down somebody else's work. If a list is taken down it stops appearing in Discover and can no longer be cloned.

Report isn't offered on **Official** lists, which are curated by us, or on your own lists.
