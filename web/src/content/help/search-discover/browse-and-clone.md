Open **Discover** from the header to browse what the community has published. You can **filter by category**, **search by publisher**, **sort by newest or most liked**, and **preview** a list's contents before taking it.

Each list has a **heart** showing how many people found it useful. Liking one helps other people find it — under the **Most liked** sort, the count decides the order, and curated **Official** lists compete on it like everything else. You can't like your own list.

![The Discover catalogue](/help-images/discover.png)

**Clone** copies a published list into your own workspace as a normal, fully editable list:

- If you already have a list with that name, the clone is auto-renamed (e.g. "Camping(1)") instead of merging.
- Recurring items are **rescheduled forward** so a cloned routine doesn't arrive instantly overdue.

Once cloned, it's completely yours — changes don't affect the published original.
