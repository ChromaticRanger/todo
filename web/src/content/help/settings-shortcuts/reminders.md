Settings → **Daily reminders** controls how proactively Stash Squirrel nudges you:

- **"Due today" pop-up on sign-in** — a summary of today's items when you open the app, optionally including overdue items.
- **Notify me when an item is due** — an in-app toast at the moment something falls due while you're using the app.
- **Daily email digest** — an email each morning (around 7am your local time) listing what's due today.

All three are independent switches, so you can have exactly as much nudging as you want — or none.
