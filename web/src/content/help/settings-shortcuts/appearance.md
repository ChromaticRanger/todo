Open the **theme picker** (palette icon in the header) to restyle the app:

- **Six colour themes** — midnight, slate, forest, sunset, rose and mono
- **Light or dark mode**, independent of the theme colour

![The theme picker](/help-images/theme-picker.png)

Your choice is saved to your account, so it follows you across browsers and devices.
