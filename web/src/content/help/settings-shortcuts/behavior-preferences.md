A few small switches in Settings tune how the app behaves:

- **Confirm before deleting** — when on, deleting an item, category or list asks first. Turn it off if you prefer speed over safety nets.
- **Completed window** — the default time range the Completed view opens with (7 / 30 / 90 days, 1 year, all time).
- **Calendar view** (Pro) — whether the Overall Schedule opens in month or week view.

Layout choices (grid vs kanban, column count) aren't in Settings — they're remembered per list, right where you switch them.
