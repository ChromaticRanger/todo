---
title: Renaming, merging and deleting categories
summary: Reshape a list's structure without touching items one by one.
plan: free
order: 3
---
Each category heading has controls to reorganise it in bulk:

- **Rename** — changes the category and moves all of its items with it.
- **Merge** — pours one category's items into another, then removes the empty one.
- **Delete** — removes the category *and its items* (you'll be asked to confirm).
- **Reorder** — drag a category by its handle to change the order sections appear in.

Because categories are derived from items, renames and merges are really bulk item updates — nothing is lost along the way.
