---
title: Moving items and categories between lists
summary: Send one item — or a whole category — to another list.
plan: free
order: 4
---
**Single item:** click the **Move to list** icon on any item's card. Pick a target list and category — or type new names to create either on the fly (subject to Free plan caps).

![The Move to list dialog](/help-images/move-dialog.png)

**Whole category:** a category can be moved to another list in one action, taking everything inside it along. You can also target a brand-new list by typing its name.

If the destination list already has a category with the same name, the arriving category is renamed **"Name(1)"** rather than being merged — so nothing gets mixed together unintentionally. Rename or merge it afterwards if combining is what you wanted.
