---
title: Categories
summary: Group items within a list under named headings.
plan: free
order: 2
---
Within a list, items are grouped into **categories** — the headed sections you see in the All view (for example *Kitchen*, *Garden*, *Paperwork* inside a "House" list).

Categories come from your items: type a new category name while adding or editing an item and the category is created automatically. You can also add an empty category up front when you're planning — click the **+ category** control in the category view (or right-click empty space) and it will sit there ready to receive items.

Items without a category gather under a general section at the top of the list.
