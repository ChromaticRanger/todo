---
title: Importing browser bookmarks
summary: Bring your existing bookmarks in from an HTML export.
plan: pro
order: 1
---
Move your browser bookmarks in bulk (up to 5 MB per file):

1. **Export** bookmarks from your browser (every major browser offers "Export bookmarks" as an HTML file).
2. In Stash Squirrel, open **Import bookmarks** from the header and upload the file.
3. **Preview** the folder structure — duplicates you already have are flagged.
4. **Map folders to lists** — each browser folder can become or join a list of your choosing.
5. Confirm to bulk-import; favicons are fetched automatically.

![The bookmark import dialog](/help-images/import-dialog.png)

Nothing is written until you confirm the mapping, so you can safely explore the preview.
