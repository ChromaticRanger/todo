title: Downloading your data
summary: Export everything in your account as a JSON file, any time.
plan: free

---
Everything you put into Stash Squirrel is yours, and you can take a copy whenever you want. Go to **Account** and click **Download my data**.

You get a single JSON file containing:

- **Every item** — todos, bookmarks, notes and events, with their list, category, due dates, priorities, repeats, colours and completion history
- **Your settings** — theme, per-list layouts, category order and preferences
- **Any lists you've published** to Discover
- **Your profile** — name, email, plan and when you joined

A few things worth knowing:

- A list that someone **shared with you** belongs to them, so its items appear in *their* export rather than yours. Items a collaborator added to a list **you** own are in yours, because they're stored against your account — they're marked with a `created_by_user_id`.
- The file is plain JSON, so any text editor will open it and any script can read it.
- Exporting doesn't change anything. You can do it as often as you like.

You don't need a Pro subscription to export, and you don't need one to keep exporting if a subscription lapses. Getting your data out isn't a paid feature.
