Open your account page from the avatar menu in the header. From there you can:

- Update your **name** and **email address**
- See your **email verification** status
- See your **plan tier** and when the account was created
- **Sign out**

Changes to your email will need re-verification, and everything on this page applies across all your devices — sessions are persistent until you sign out.
