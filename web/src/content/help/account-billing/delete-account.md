---
title: Deleting your account
summary: Permanently remove your account and everything in it.
plan: free
order: 3
---
You can permanently delete your account from the account page.

Deletion **cascades to everything you own** — all lists, todos, bookmarks, notes, events, settings and published Discover entries are removed. This is irreversible, so you'll be asked to confirm.

If you have an active Pro subscription, cancel it first via the billing portal so you're not charged after the account is gone.
