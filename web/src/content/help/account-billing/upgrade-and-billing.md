---
title: Upgrading and managing billing
summary: Go Pro, switch between monthly and annual, and find your invoices.
plan: free
order: 2
---
Upgrade to **Pro** from your account page at any time — choose monthly or annual billing and check out securely through **Stripe**. Pro unlocks immediately once payment completes; no restart needed.

Everything billing-related afterwards happens in the **Stripe billing portal**, linked from your account page:

- Update your payment method
- Switch between monthly and annual
- Download invoices
- Cancel — you keep Pro until the end of the paid period

Your plan status updates automatically; if you cancel and later resubscribe, your data has been there all along.
