---
title: Saving bookmarks
summary: Keep URLs as list items with automatically fetched favicons.
plan: free
order: 1
---
Click **Add → Bookmark** (or press **Alt+B**), paste the URL and give it a title. The site's **favicon is fetched automatically** so the card is recognisable at a glance.

Bookmarks belong to a list, can be grouped into categories, and can be found later with global search (Pro). Priorities, due dates and repeats are todo-only — a bookmark is just a saved link.

Two faster ways to capture bookmarks:

- The **browser extension** saves the page you're on without opening the app — see *Import & Browser Extension*.
- **Bookmark import** (Pro) brings in your existing browser bookmarks in bulk.
