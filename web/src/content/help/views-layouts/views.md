Every list can be looked at through six views, switchable just under the list tabs:

![The view switcher with live count badges](/help-images/view-switcher.png)

- **All** — every pending item, grouped by category. This is the default.
- **Today** — todos due before the end of today.
- **Week** — todos due in the next 7 days.
- **Month** — todos due in the next 30 days.
- **Overdue** — pending todos whose due date has passed.
- **Completed** — what you've finished, over a selectable window (7 / 30 / 90 days, 1 year, or all time).

The time-windowed views carry live **count badges**, so you can see at a glance how much is due today or overdue without switching. Snoozed items are hidden from All but still shown in the time views so deadlines never disappear entirely.
