title: Drag & drop
summary: Reorder and reorganise everything directly.

---
Most organisation is direct manipulation — grab the **drag handle** on a card or heading:

- **Items** — reorder within a category, or drag into a different category (in kanban, drag between columns).
- **Categories** — drag to reorder the sections of a list.
- **List tabs** — drag to reorder the tabs themselves.

On the calendar (Pro), dragging works on the time grid too — drag on empty space to create an event, and drag events to move them.
